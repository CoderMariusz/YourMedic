## BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS

### PATH TO PROFITABILITY

**Current Model Shows:**
- Year 3 still has significant losses (-€3.67M)
- Infrastructure costs scale with revenue (Stripe fees are 54% of Year 3 costs!)
- Need to reassess pricing or reduce infrastructure costs

**Break-Even Requirements:**
To break even at Month 36 scale:
- **Current:** €712k revenue vs €4.38M costs
- **Need:** 6x revenue increase OR significant cost reduction

**Revised Strategy Options:**

1. **Higher Pricing (Premium Model)**
   - Increase booking fee: €10 → €15 (+50%)
   - Increase doctor sub: €35 → €50 (+43%)
   - New Month 36 revenue: €712k × 1.46 = **€1.04M** (still not enough)

2. **Commission Model (Marketplace)**
   - Take 10% of appointment value (avg €80 = €8 commission)
   - Month 36: 5,285 appointments × €8 = €42k/month = **€505k/year**
   - PLUS doctor subs: €1,220 × €35 × 12 = **€512k/year**
   - **Total: €1.02M** (still short of €4.38M)

3. **B2B Enterprise Model (Recommended)**
   - Sell directly to clinics: €500-2,000/month per clinic
   - Target: 200 clinics by Month 36 × €1,000 avg = **€200k/month = €2.4M/year**
   - PLUS patient booking fees: 5,285 × €10 × 12 = **€635k/year**
   - **Total: €3.03M** (68% of costs - getting closer!)

4. **Hybrid Model (B2B + B2C)**
   - 150 clinic contracts: €1,000/month × 150 × 12 = **€1.8M**
   - 8,000 appointments/month × €12 fee × 12 = **€1.15M**
   - 500 individual doctors × €50/month × 12 = **€300k**
   - **Total: €3.25M** (74% cost coverage)

**Reality Check:**
To reach true break-even (€4.38M revenue) at Month 36 scale, need:
- **300 clinic contracts** at €1,000/month = €3.6M
- **5,000 appointments/month** × €12 × 12 = €720k
- **Total: €4.32M** ✅ BREAK-EVEN

## KEY FINANCIAL METRICS

### UNIT ECONOMICS (Year 3 Mature State)

**Patient LTV (Lifetime Value):**
- Avg appointments per year: 2.5
- Booking fee per appointment: €12
- Avg patient lifespan: 5 years
- **Patient LTV = 2.5 × €12 × 5 = €150**

**Patient CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):**
- Year 3 marketing spend: €660k
- New patients acquired: ~12,000 (5,000 appointments/month × 40% new patients × 12 months / 2.5 appointments per patient)
- **Patient CAC = €660k / 12,000 = €55**

**LTV:CAC Ratio = €150 / €55 = 2.7** ✅ (Target: >3.0, acceptable: >2.0)

---

**Doctor LTV:**
- Avg subscription: €50/month
- Avg doctor retention: 24 months
- **Doctor LTV = €50 × 24 = €1,200**

**Doctor CAC:**
- Onboarding incentives + sales: €500/doctor
- **LTV:CAC Ratio = €1,200 / €500 = 2.4** ✅

---

**Clinic LTV (B2B):**
- Avg contract: €1,000/month
- Avg clinic retention: 36 months
- **Clinic LTV = €1,000 × 36 = €36,000**

**Clinic CAC:**
- B2B sales cost: €3,000/clinic (sales team + onboarding)
- **LTV:CAC Ratio = €36,000 / €3,000 = 12.0** ✅✅ (Excellent!)
